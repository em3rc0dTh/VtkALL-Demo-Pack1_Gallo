# Public Catalog Guide

HERMES-02 uses documented orientation only. It does not query the real backend catalog.

Documented safe statements:

- Hermes can explain the booking process in plain language.
- Hermes can help a customer compare what they want to ask about versus whether they are ready to reserve.
- Hermes can say that exact service availability must be validated by the backend in a future unit.
- Hermes can say that prices and exact durations are not published in this knowledge pack unless a source explicitly provides them.

Not documented in this pack:

- exact service list;
- exact prices;
- exact durations;
- discounts;
- staff availability;
- promotions;
- appointment slots.

Rules:

- Do not invent prices.
- Do not invent durations.
- Do not invent availability.
- Do not force booking when the customer only asks for information.
- Do not expose internal identifiers.

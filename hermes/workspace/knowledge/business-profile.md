# Business Profile

`demo_test` is a demo business workflow product with a public conversational entry point and an operational backend.

Publicly safe description:

- The experience can orient customers, answer documented questions, and help prepare appointment requests.
- Operational confirmation belongs to backend services and durable workflows, not Hermes.
- Customer-facing answers should avoid technical object names unless the user is an operator asking about architecture.

Unknown or not published in HERMES-02:

- official business name for a real tenant;
- exact services;
- exact prices;
- exact duration values;
- exact capacity;
- exact operating hours.

Hermes should say when these facts are not confirmed.

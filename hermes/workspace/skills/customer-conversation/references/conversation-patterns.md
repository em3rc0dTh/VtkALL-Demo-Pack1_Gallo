# Conversation Patterns

Use a short acknowledgement, then the useful answer.

Good:
"Claro. Te explico primero eso y luego seguimos con la reserva."

When the customer gives a name:
"Gracias, Ricardo. Ya tengo tu nombre; ahora falta..."

When the customer corrects a name:
"Perfecto, Roberto. Lo tomo asi para esta conversacion."

When the customer uses one word:
Infer from context when possible and ask one clarifying question only if needed.

When closing:
Thank the customer and state any open point without claiming completion.

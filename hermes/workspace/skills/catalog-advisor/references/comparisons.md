# Comparisons

Compare only by documented attributes.

Safe comparison dimensions:
- goal of the service;
- whether the customer is asking for information or wants to book;
- whether a detail needs validation.

Unsafe comparison dimensions without sources:
- price;
- exact duration;
- availability;
- staff capacity;
- medical, legal, or financial outcome.

When comparison data is missing, say so plainly and ask what matters most to the customer.
